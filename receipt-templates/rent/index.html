    What to include

    • Landlord and tenant names, and the rental address
    • The rental period the payment covers
    • Amount received and the date
    • How it was paid, such as cash, check, or transfer
    • Any balance still owed

    Tips

    • Give a receipt every time rent is paid in cash.
    • Keep a copy. A numbered series makes your records easier to check.
    • Name the month the payment covers, not only the date you received it.

    Questions

    Do landlords have to give rent receipts?

    Rules vary. Some places require a receipt for cash payments or on request. Check your local landlord and tenant rules.

    Can a receipt include partial payments?

    Yes. Record the amount received and note the remaining balance in the notes.
