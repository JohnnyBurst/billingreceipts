    What to include

    • Customer name and the address of the job
    • The rooms or surfaces painted
    • Prep work such as sanding, patching and taping
    • Labor as hours or a flat price per room
    • Paint and supplies, with brand and color if useful
    • Any deposit already received

    Tips

    • Reference the quote so the scope matches what was agreed.
    • List extra coats or repairs as separate lines.
    • Include the color names and paint brand for the customer's records.

    Questions

    Should paint be a separate line?

    Usually yes, especially if you charge the customer at cost plus a markup. Flat per-room prices can include materials, but say so.

    When should I invoice a painting job?

    Many painters take a deposit before starting and invoice the balance when the job is finished and the customer has walked through it.
