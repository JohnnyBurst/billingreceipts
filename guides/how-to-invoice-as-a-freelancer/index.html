How to invoice as a freelancer

Freelancers invoice directly, which means getting the details right yourself. A professional invoice gets paid faster and makes tax time easier.

Set your terms first

Agree rates, deposits, payment terms and late fees in writing before you start. Put them on every invoice.

What to include

  • Your name or business name and contact details
  • Client name and billing contact
  • Invoice number and date
  • Clear descriptions of the work, with hours or fixed prices
  • Total, due date and how to pay

How often to bill

Bill at project milestones for larger work, monthly for ongoing work, and on delivery for small jobs. Ask for a deposit on new clients.

Keep records

Save every invoice and receipt. They help with taxes, disputes and applying for loans.

Questions

Do freelancers need to charge tax?

It depends on where you and your clients are and what you sell. Ask a local accountant or check your tax authority.

What if a client does not pay?

Send a reminder, then a firmer notice. Keep the contract and invoices handy. Consider a collections step only after that.
