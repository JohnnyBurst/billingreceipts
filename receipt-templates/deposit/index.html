    What to include

    • What the deposit is for and the total price, if known
    • The amount received and the date
    • How it was paid
    • The balance due and when it is due
    • Whether the deposit is refundable and under what conditions

    Tips

    • State the refund terms plainly. Vague terms cause most deposit disputes.
    • Reference the quote or contract the deposit applies to.
    • Show the deposit as already paid on the final invoice.

    Questions

    Is a deposit refundable?

    That depends on your agreement and local rules. Write the terms you have agreed on in the notes.

    How is a deposit different from a down payment?

    In everyday use the terms overlap. Both are paid in advance and credited against the final price.
