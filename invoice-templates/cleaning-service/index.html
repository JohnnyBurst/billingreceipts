    What to include

    • Service date and the address that was cleaned
    • Type of clean, such as standard, deep clean, or move-out
    • Flat price or hours at your hourly rate
    • Add-ons like inside oven, windows, or fridge as separate lines
    • Payment terms for recurring clients, such as due on receipt

    Tips

    • Keep one invoice per visit for one-time jobs and one per month for weekly clients.
    • List supplies separately only if you charge for them.
    • Add a short thank-you line. Repeat clients notice it.

    Questions

    Do I charge by the hour or a flat rate?

    Either works. Flat rates are easier for clients to budget. Hourly rates protect you on homes with unpredictable conditions. Many cleaners quote a flat rate for the first visit and adjust after seeing the home.

    How do I bill a weekly client?

    Most cleaners send one invoice per visit or one monthly invoice listing each visit date. Put the dates in the line descriptions so the client can match them to their calendar.
