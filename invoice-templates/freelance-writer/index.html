    What to include

    • Title or description of each piece, with the publication date or the project name
    • Word count or the flat fee for each piece
    • Rate per word, per piece, or per hour
    • Any kill fee or rush fee, with the reason
    • How and when you expect payment

    Tips

    • Match the invoice description to the title in the contract or assignment email.
    • Include the editor's name and the assignment date in the notes.
    • Ask whether the publisher needs a W-9 or a vendor form before your first payment.

    Questions

    How should I number invoices?

    Any consistent system works. Many freelancers use a year plus a running number, like 2026-014. See our guide on how to write an invoice.

    What if a client pays late?

    State your terms on the invoice and send a polite reminder a few days after the due date. Late fees can be added only if your agreement allows it and local rules permit.
