Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-15. This is a plain-language summary. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it.

The generator

The invoice and receipt generator runs in your browser. If you don’t sign in, the details you type are not sent to our servers.

Accounts

You can use the generator without an account, and nothing you type leaves your browser. If you choose to sign in, we store your email address and the invoices, receipts and clients you save, so you can open them on other devices. We use Supabase to provide sign-in and storage. Saved documents are visible only to your account. Sign out any time, and delete a saved document to remove it.

Payments

If you buy Pro, Stripe handles the payment. We never see or store your card details. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs so we know which plan you’re on.

Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep documents you save without an account and to keep you signed in. Clearing this site’s data removes them. If we add analytics, we will list the provider here and describe what it collects.
