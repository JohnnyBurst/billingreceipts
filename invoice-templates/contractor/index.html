    What to include

    • Your business name, contact details, and license number if your area requires one on invoices
    • Client name and the job site address
    • Labor as hours at an hourly rate, or as a flat price per task
    • Materials on their own lines, with your markup if you charge one
    • Any deposit already received, so the balance due is clear

    Tips

    • Reference the quote or contract number so the invoice ties back to the agreed scope.
    • Bill change orders as separate lines with the date they were approved.
    • State when the balance is due and which payment methods you accept.

    Questions

    Should I charge sales tax on a contractor invoice?

    It depends on where you work and what you sold. Some places tax materials, some tax labor, some tax neither. Check with a local accountant or your state tax office, then enter the rate that applies in the tax field.

    Can I invoice before the job is finished?

    Yes. Many contractors invoice a deposit up front and bill the rest at milestones or on completion. Say which one this invoice is in the notes.
