    What to include

    • Buyer and seller names
    • A clear description of the item, with serial number if it has one
    • Sale price and date
    • Payment method
    • Condition, such as sold as is
    • Signatures, if you want both sides to sign

    Tips

    • Include serial numbers for electronics, tools and bikes.
    • State sold as is if you give no warranty.
    • Both sides should keep a copy.

    Questions

    Does it replace a title or bill of sale?

    No. For vehicles, boats and similar items you may need a title transfer or a specific form. Check your local rules.

    Should I include ID details?

    Names and contact details are usually enough. Only add ID numbers if you really need them and keep them private.
