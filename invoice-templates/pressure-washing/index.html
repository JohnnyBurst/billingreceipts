    What to include

    • Customer name and service address
    • Surfaces cleaned, such as driveway, patio or siding
    • Area in square feet or a flat price per surface
    • Add-ons like sealing or soft washing
    • Travel fee if applicable
    • Payment terms

    Tips

    • Put square footage on the invoice if you priced by area.
    • Note any damage seen before you started.
    • Offer a discount for recurring service and show it as a line.

    Questions

    Per square foot or per job?

    Both are common. Per square foot is fair for large areas. A flat price is simpler for small, clear jobs. State which you used on the invoice.

    Should I list chemicals?

    Only if you charge for them. If they are included in the price, you can leave them off.
