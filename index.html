    Questions

    Is BillingReceipts really free?

    Yes. Creating invoices and receipts and downloading them as PDFs is free, with no account needed. Signing in to sync up to 10 saved documents is also free. A Pro plan with unlimited sync, saved clients, and your logo is planned.

    Where is my data stored?

    Nothing you type into the generator is sent to a server. The document is built in your browser.

    What is the difference between an invoice and a receipt?

    An invoice asks for payment and lists what is owed. A receipt confirms that payment was made, so it shows the payment method and a paid mark.

    Is this tax or legal advice?

    No. Tax rules and required invoice wording vary by place. Check with an accountant for your situation.
