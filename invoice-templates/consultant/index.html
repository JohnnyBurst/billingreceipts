What to include

  • The project or engagement name and any purchase order number
  • The billing period
  • Hours at your rate, or the fixed fee and milestone it covers
  • Reimbursable expenses as separate lines
  • Payment terms such as net 30 and how to pay

Tips

  • Put the client's PO number in the notes. Many companies will not pay without it.
  • Attach a short summary of work done if hours are large.
  • Send the invoice to the accounts payable contact, not only your project sponsor.

Questions

What payment terms should I use?

Net 30 is common with larger companies. Smaller clients often pay faster with net 14 or due on receipt. See our guide on invoice payment terms for the tradeoffs.

Should expenses be on the same invoice?

Usually yes, as separate lines. Keep receipts in case the client asks.
